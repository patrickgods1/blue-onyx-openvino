Benchmark

Runs each model on each device over the chosen datasets with the production pipeline (decode, preprocess, inference, postprocess), grades accuracy (AP@0.5 against ground truth, or against a reference model where a dataset has none) and speed (full request p50), and checks that every device detects the same objects as the CPU. The recommended device of a model is its best-graded device whose detections agree with the CPU; within 5% of the best speed the configured device is kept.
